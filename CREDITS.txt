Allied Front
============

An original browser game. It borrows the look and feel of early-2000s
WWII first-person shooters (low-poly models, low-resolution textures,
fog, a muted palette) but does not use any assets, names, levels, or
logos from Medal of Honor or any other commercial game.

Art
---
3D models, textures, and the helmet-and-star app icon were generated
procedurally for this project (no third-party packs).

Audio
-----
All sound effects are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.
No sampled commercial game audio is used.

Fonts
-----
System UI fonts only (Trebuchet MS, Segoe UI, Gill Sans, sans-serif).
