# Allied Front

A small, kid-friendly browser FPS in an early-2000s style. Clear a foggy French village and reach the radio. There is no gore.

This is an original game. It is inspired by the look and feel of early-2000s WWII shooters, but it does not use any of their assets, names, levels, or logos.

## Play

Open `index.html` locally, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo (source: `/`, root of the branch).

Desktop: WASD, mouse look (click to capture), left click fire, right click aim, R reload, 1/2 switch weapons, Shift sprint, Esc pause.

Phone and tablet: left stick to move, drag the right side to look, FIRE buttons on both sides (the right one is the main button), AIM and R, pause with ❚❚.
