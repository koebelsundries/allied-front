CONTROLS

Computer

  • W A S D — move
  • Mouse — look (click to capture)
  • Left click — fire
  • Right click — aim
  • R — reload
  • 1 / 2 — rifle / SMG
  • Shift — sprint
  • Esc — pause

Phone & tablet

  • Left stick — move
  • Drag the right side — look
  • FIRE buttons on both sides (right is the main one; drag it to look while firing)
  • AIM toggles sights — then drag to look. R reloads.
  • Push the stick all the way to sprint
  • 🏠 or ❚❚ — pause menu
